## Animation direction, recorded before keyframes

Idle snap: sovereign stillness, then impossible observation. Body frozen; head holds front, snaps exactly 90 degrees sideways in one frame, holds, then returns by another abrupt quarter turn. No breathing/bobbing. Three-quarter camera must expose the head opening during yaw.

Detached strikes: each hand retracts slightly and turns its palm, then launches independently toward the viewer, arrests hard, and withdraws slowly. The supporting arm remains still. Hands retain their disconnected wrist gap. Separate left/right clips preserve asymmetric digits.

Shell opening: left and right torso plates separate sideways, the left leads slightly; inward-facing edges reveal true open space. Hold the opened silhouette, then close with a decisive stop. Legs do not bob with the shells.

Teleport compression: bend knees and lift legs into the central void while forearms fold and free hands gather near the chest; head sinks slightly and crown follows later. Hold a compact suspended pose, release to the original stance. Do not scale the entire creature or add rings/particles.
