# Candy King animation briefs

## Character read

The creature is a tall, dry orchard skeleton carrying a hungry candy maw in its abdomen. It does not move like a balanced human. Its rigid trunk leads, the very long limbs arrive late, root feet search for purchase, and the pumpkin head counterbalances with small suspicious turns. The supplied front and three-quarter reference owns the anatomy and silhouette; external scarecrow images are technical proposals only.

## `animation.candy_king.idle`

- Intent: hold an unnervingly patient ambush pose while the abdominal maw quietly reveals that the body is alive.
- Action: the torso makes one slow forward inhale and returns; the head counters by a few degrees, hands sag after the body, and the back branches settle last.
- Key poses: neutral hold at 0.0 s; shallow chest lift and head counter-tilt at 0.7 s; maw half-open with wrists lagging at 1.35 s; quiet recovery at 2.1 s; exact loop closure at 2.8 s.
- Rhythm/contact: most of the loop is a hold. Both root feet remain planted; no whole-body bobbing.
- Camera readability: the pumpkin face and vertical belly mouth remain readable from front and three-quarter views.
- End state: exact neutral pose, ready to branch into locomotion or attack.

## `animation.candy_king.walk`

- Intent: cross ground like an over-tall marionette whose limbs are pulled by its spine.
- Action: alternating long steps with a small pelvis shift, opposite arm swing, delayed hands, and a restrained head counter-turn.
- Key poses: left contact at 0.0 s; passing pose at 0.3 s; right contact at 0.6 s; second passing pose at 0.9 s; loop closure at 1.2 s.
- Rhythm/contact: feet trade support without sliding below the ground plane; knees bend only enough for the root toes to clear. The torso never becomes a buoyant human jog.
- Secondary motion: forearms trail upper arms, hands trail forearms, and the back branches answer the chest after each contact.
- Camera readability: the alternating leg silhouette must survive front and side views.
- End state: exact first contact pose.

## `animation.candy_king.maw_bite`

- Intent: make the abdominal mouth, not the pumpkin head, deliver the threat.
- Action: a short backward coil, sudden chest thrust and vertical maw snap, then a heavy recoil that drags the hands forward before recovery.
- Key poses: watchful neutral at 0.0 s; compressed anticipation at 0.22 s; maw fully open at 0.44 s; bite impact at 0.58 s; overshoot at 0.76 s; controlled recovery at 1.15 s.
- Rhythm/contact: root feet stay planted and the pelvis absorbs the impulse. The fast bite is surrounded by longer anticipation and recovery.
- Secondary motion: head lags the thrust, wrists and fingers arrive after impact, branches whip once and settle.
- Camera readability: the open dark cavity, teeth and colored candy remain visible in the front three-quarter combat camera.
- End state: neutral stance, no hidden root motion.
