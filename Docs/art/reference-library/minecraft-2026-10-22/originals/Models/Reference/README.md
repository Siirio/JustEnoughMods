## What the user explicitly selected

1. **Coherent form transitions:** previous models looked like inconsistent, unrelated chunks of cuboids; these references show related masses, tapers, overlaps and direction changes that read as a continuous form even though they are built from cuboids. Keep crisp Minecraft-compatible angular construction; do not equate flow with smooth shading or high-poly geometry.
2. **Intentional painted materials — highest priority:** previous textures were dull, calculated geometric patterns with little artistic intent or transition. Compose expressive color/value masses, directional pixel clusters, purposeful highlights/shadows and material patterns that support the specific form and continue across relevant surfaces. Do not substitute random rectangles, repeated bands or global saturation increases for painting.

The user supplied this extraction directly; no repeat calibration question is needed. These are positive examples for the two selected qualities, not blanket approval of every subject, palette, theme, proportion, outline, glowing effect or level of detail. Dark/grey references demonstrate that expressive painting can use restrained color. Static images do not establish animation cadence or runtime quality.
