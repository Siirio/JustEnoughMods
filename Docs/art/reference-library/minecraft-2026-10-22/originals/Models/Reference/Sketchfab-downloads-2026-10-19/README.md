- Whale: the tapered trunk, narrowing tail attachment and broad angled fins create one connected silhouette. Dark teal upper surfaces and pale underside describe large anatomical regions; directional dark line clusters add local surface information. Practice transferable taper, fitted attachment and large value groups; the specific whale palette is not a universal preference.
- Kraken: bent, tapering tentacle chains vary their outward directions and remain attached to a shared body mass. Purple-to-blue progression supports appendage direction, while the large dark face cavity remains a focal value shape. Static export does not prove tentacle motion.
- Dimetrodon: a large body, projecting snout, short weight-bearing legs and thin dorsal sail produce distinct mass roles using only 20 exported meshes. The 128x128 atlas includes body shading, sail marks and separate mouth/eye details. High mesh count is not necessary for identity.
- Red diesel switcher: large red body panels, quieter grey roof/rails and dark undercarriage establish material/value organization. Thin rail geometry supplies silhouette and open space; texture supplies vents, panel seams and small labels. Its 1024x1024 image is sparsely occupied; atlas dimensions alone are not a useful detail target.
- Pitlord: the large blade, heavy body and smaller horns/spines establish unequal scale roles. Green accents contrast with subdued blue-grey flesh and dark red armor. Complex subjects can use dense detail, but this example does not authorize that density for every subject. 474 exported meshes and ten clips are inventory facts, not performance or artistic acceptance.
- Wands: the actual atlas is only 32x32. Broad purple, cyan, warm wood and gold regions remain readable on the model. Three differently sized head constructions create related identities. Texture resolution should follow readable material and shape decisions rather than be increased automatically.
- Witch items: a broad cauldron rim, recessed liquid, fitted side hardware, narrow broom handle and bundled bristles give each part a structural role. Subdued purple-grey vessel, brown wood, straw and bright green liquid separate materials. Texture marks follow each region rather than one stamped pattern. Three atlases are 32x32, 64x64 and 32x32.
- Christmas scene: the atlas contains distinct scene materials and an additional 32x32 image. Full scene composition needs closer framing before drawing strong geometry conclusions.
