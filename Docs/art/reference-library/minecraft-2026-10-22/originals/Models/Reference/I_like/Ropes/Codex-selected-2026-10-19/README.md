# Minecraft cuboid ropes — selected study references, 2026-10-19

User explicitly prohibited smooth objects and requested rope research plus downloaded examples under I_like. This folder follows that requested destination. Classification: CODEX_SELECTED_POSITIVE_TECHNIQUE_REFERENCE, not a claimed user vote on these artists or whole objects. The user's established extraction is angular Minecraft construction with believable load/contact and texture-authored fine detail. These selected examples are proposed implementations of that direction; no approval question is required just to collect/study them.

## Actual images inspected

1. **Low-poly Decorated Torii Gate — nin / voxelly_ninja.** https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-decorated-torii-gate-a730c4df623d433189be7c341972cea0 . The artist explicitly reports 20 cubes for the whole object. The rope reads as a broad low angular sag with a few changes of direction and hanging rectangular fringes. Study few fitted segments and texture, not a cylindrical spline or individual helical strands. It is a slack decorative span, so its sag is not automatically appropriate for the goblin's loaded spans.
2. **Mineclub | Well — V-Artistry.** https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/272a84bfdca542b985cb02073d140247 . Actual preview shows a compact angular post wrap, an attached angled span and a bucket line. Study visible square wrapping and clear structural connection. Preview alone does not prove the source topology or gravity; do not copy any unsupported bucket arrangement as a physics contract.
3. **Wooden Well — Workshop Six.** https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/c8a16cb516174cf792ca3aae3caa67e2 . Straight narrow bucket rope and restrained square-section wraps on the spindle; painted diagonal light/dark marks do work that does not require modeling strands. Whole well palette/proportions remain unclassified.

Original 1920px public preview images and API metadata are preserved. provenance.json records author, URL and hash. reference-board.png is an uncropped review composition with credits. The API did not expose downloadable native models for these three, so these are downloaded reference images, not falsely claimed bbmodel downloads. No production reuse license was established for their assets. They are reference-only.

A fourth Wishing Well by Elsewhere was inspected but not promoted: its winding/coil construction needs closer topology evidence and is less clear as a cuboid-only rope study. It remains in the temporary research staging folder, not I_like.
