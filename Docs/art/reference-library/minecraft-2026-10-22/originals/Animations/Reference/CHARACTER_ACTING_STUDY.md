## Applying the study before art direction

Write what the viewer should understand about this creature, what it wants in this moment, and which anatomical/prop habit can reveal that. Choose relevant references by those needs. Name the concrete movement evidence borrowed from each and any deliberately different interpretation. Build the per-clip creative prompt before keying, then judge actual movement against the intended character reading as well as contact, weight and silhouette. A mechanically correct pose sequence with no readable intent does not satisfy the art direction.

This requirement does not force a comic incident into every idle. The mage's quiet absorption, the wave's social purpose, the door's comic consequence and the dragonfly's mechanical articulation remain distinct useful approaches.
