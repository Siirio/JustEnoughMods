## CHAR01 — golden dragonfly

**User direction:** use for mechanical jerks, with a possible later application to horror jerks. This is approval of those extracted movement qualities; horror is a proposed future use, not a claim that this clip itself establishes a horror character. Gold is an identifying description, not a universal animation palette.

**Observed performance:** [0–1.53 s](Review/CHAR01_detail_01.jpg) keeps the wings largely extended in one arrangement, with small changing body/underside appendage configurations. [1.60–3.13 s](Review/CHAR01_detail_02.jpg) continues that comparatively held configuration, followed by a conspicuous framing/scale change around 2.93 s. Do not count that presentation change as a bodily jolt. [3.20–4.73 s](Review/CHAR01_detail_03.jpg) and [4.80–5.47 s](Review/CHAR01_detail_04.jpg) repeatedly switch between widely separated wing angles; rigid body mass stays the visual anchor. The visible rhythm differs between the quiet opening and active-wing passage. Sampling fast wings can alias phase and cannot prove the original interpolation is Step.

**Character reading — agent synthesis:** the restrained chassis and brisk articulated changes can suggest a mechanism operating in discrete commands. The mechanical impression depends on purposeful joints and starts/stops, rather than arbitrary jitter on every bone. The insect's body plan supplies a specific motion vocabulary rather than a generic humanoid shake.

**Scoped creative use:** for a mechanism, choose one actuator to engage, give it a readable abrupt displacement/stop, and let any attached response respect stiffness. For a future horror brief, a deliberate still interval interrupted by an unexpected, directed head/limb correction is a candidate to audition. That irregular horror rhythm is an adaptation proposal, not measured behaviour recovered from this video. Preserve readability; do not turn all idle motion into constant random noise or transfer flapping cadence directly to a grounded monster.

## CHAR02 — fat chicken

**User direction:** a highly creative, creature-specific idle: lazy and irritable, belly gurgling, legs stretching and striking; the combined movement conveys a somewhat foolish bird. Preserve this intended role and the connection between physical business and personality. Do not reduce the reference to generic breathing or copy its leg action into every creature.

**Observed performance:** [0–3.83 s](Review/CHAR02_detail_01.jpg) begins with empty/tiny framing, then a zoom/orbit into the seated bulky figure. Around 1.0–1.33 s the side appendages open and come down. Head/body tip and return while feet project forward. [4.0–7.83 s](Review/CHAR02_detail_02.jpg) includes another open-and-lower gesture, a forward head/body inclination around 4.67–5.83 s, then an upright seated recovery near 6.33 s. [8.0–11.83 s](Review/CHAR02_detail_03.jpg) has a comparatively quiet stretch before the head tips forward near 10.33–10.50 s; around 10.67–11.83 s the figure reclines, opens its appendages and separates/extends the feet. [12.0–14.67 s](Review/CHAR02_detail_04.jpg) alternates a brief upright recovery with another reclined, feet-forward gesture near 13.0–14.0 s and returns nearer the seated baseline by the end. Exact foot-strike contact is not resolved by these samples.

**Character reading:** laziness, anger, belly gurgling and foolishness are the user's explicit interpretation. Visually, a large seated mass does little locomotor work while the smaller feet and upper appendages make petulant bursts; conspicuous effort produces small results. Quiet time makes these incidents feel like habits of this particular creature. Belly gurgling is retained as the user's intended cue; no audio gurgle or exact belly deformation method was independently established.

**Scoped creative use — agent synthesis:** write a short behavioural sentence such as “too lazy to stand, it shifts its belly, stretches its feet, gets annoyed and kicks, then slumps back.” Make the belly/feet/head reactions causal and asymmetrical where useful. Derive the action from anatomy and temperament; choose a different habit for a proud, vigilant or predatory creature. Split sustained seated idle from occasional incidents as appropriate to the eventual consumer. Neither the 14.8-second recording nor its camera movements define a required loop.
