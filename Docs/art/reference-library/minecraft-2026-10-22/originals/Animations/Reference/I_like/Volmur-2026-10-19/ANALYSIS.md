## Reusable production sequence

1. Select the reference whose actual action matches the task; write the movement sentence. A wave, idle, floating soul and treatment sequence have different contracts.
2. Block intention, primary extreme/contact and result as readable whole-body or hand/prop poses. Establish the main action before decoration.
3. Allocate preparation, fast action, pauses and consequence where the action needs them. Do not add anticipation to every tiny motion by habit.
4. Solve support feet and hand/prop contact before secondary motion. Use the late leg settle as an observed example; hair/appendage phase numbers remain unmeasured.
5. Compare with a stable camera. Preserve purposeful asymmetry, quiet supporting regions and the focal action rather than moving every bone in phase.
6. Explicitly choose one-shot, entry-plus-loop or loop. Neither the greeting's rest-to-wave recording nor the door collapse proves a seamless loop.
