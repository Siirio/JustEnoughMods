## What these images tell us

The user's labels and the earlier inspected source study establish a preference
for authored pixel materials, pictorial subject hierarchy, integrated controls,
crafted frame silhouettes, readable state differences and deliberate spatial
grouping. Rejected JEM examples show that brown rectangles, thin copper borders,
small item icons and more text do not by themselves meet the desired finish.
Some professionally finished vector emblems, ornate HUDs, book treatments and
infographic posters are also negative: “fantasy,” “Minecraft” or “illustrated”
alone is not a sufficiently specific target.

Do not interpret this as a universal ban on brown, gold, books, radial layouts,
glow or illustrations. Compare the actual relevant positive and negative files
for the current screen's purpose. GUI layout and material taste do not establish
race-VFX, entity-model or observed animation preferences.

The [original visual study](<../Forge-1.20.1-jem-smp-screens/Knowledge-sources/01_user_labeled_gui_vfx_reference_study_2026-10-16.md>)
