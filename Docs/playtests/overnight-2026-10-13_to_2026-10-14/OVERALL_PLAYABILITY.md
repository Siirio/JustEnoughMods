# Candid playability assessment

JEM currently presents much more promised progression than this newcomer can use.
The ten-profession overview is clear enough atGUI4, but the actual Architect/Farmer
trees expose unavailable nodes, repeat their titles as explanations and overflow
fixed cards. A player cannot turn an impressive tree into an understandable plan.
The next design pass should ship fewer fully working actions with clear earning,
parent/unlock and unavailable reasons, rather than decorating incomplete promises.

The strongest verified loop is physical resource -> native merchant -> Coin wallet:
inventory and money movement are visible and the native stock owner correction now
behaves once. The presentation is weak: an icon/price hides direction and amount,
generic no-market tooltip contradicts a supported trade, and instructions cross
into JEI. Explicit give/take rows and live wallet/stock state would make the same
server-authoritative mechanic understandable. The Human refund emerald mint is a
real cross-mod resource defect, not merely an ugly screen.

Native world movement, small crafting and harvest interactions worked in non-op
Survival. The sky-village view is attractive, but first-hour onboarding relies on
commands and hidden chat; Town gives no visible founding route, and the quest book
refuses after clean login until operator reload. A newcomer should discover role,
work, shelter, food and a first useful goal without having to infer protocol state.

Inventory avatar invisibility, overlapping race prose/footer and long SMP hover
lines are conspicuous visual failures. The art direction concept is unapproved;
no production UI/EMF repair was installed. Shader-on60FPS versus-off134FPS is one
same-position laptop sample; it does not prove universal performance or balance.

No combat satisfaction, boss choreography, event fairness, six-day settlement
output, social emergence or long-term grind judgment is justified by this run.
These are important explicit gaps, not silently passed boxes. Historical JEMServer
results are generation-qualified after the full-JAR setup error was discovered.
