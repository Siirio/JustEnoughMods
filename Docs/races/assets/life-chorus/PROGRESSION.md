# Three sequential upgrades — suggestions for remaining races

Bloom is implemented as base→I→II→III, per explicit user instruction. Its F6 unlock
buttons are sandbox controls, not a proposal for live progression costs. Other
race upgrades below are **proposals only**, not active changes.

| Race | I | II | III |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ancestors | Quiet healing echo catches allies arriving just after the main wave, without healing an already-treated entity twice | One miniature ward answers the most injured nearby ally/mount; keep a visible link to that recipient | Guardian prioritizes protecting a warded ally when the caster is safe; existing25% caster reduction stays baseline |
| Void | Departure cut can be used once to return within3s; does not reset the main cast | During the opening pause, choose which side of the target the exit faces | One controlled follow-up cut to a second marked target, with a shared cast budget and no recursive refresh |
| Sculk | First pressure front interrupts a hostile wind-up briefly | A weaker echo slows instead of repeating the full4-heart hit | Reflect the echo from a nearby wall into a chosen sector; one damage cap per target, so enclosed rooms do not multiply damage |
| Nether | Steer the distant eruption during its wind-up | Delay ignition of the ground scar and choose when to deny that corridor | Hit your own growth with the И finisher to break it into a directed magma burst; consumes the growth instead of spawning unlimited fire walls |

These change tactical decisions rather than merely increasing visual size or
damage. Durations, cost, cooldown, unlock source and boss exceptions need a later
design decision. No new grind or purchases are invented here.

Balance concern to review: fully upgraded Bloom combines6 initial +12 scheduled
constriction damage over4s + poison +4.5s complete suppression. That is severe
against ordinary mobs. Preserve the explicitly requested numbers; a separate
boss-control rule would be a proposed change, not an unannounced nerf.
