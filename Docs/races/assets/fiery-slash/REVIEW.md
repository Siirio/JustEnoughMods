## R4 — ART_CANDIDATE, not reference-level acceptance

Evidence: r4/contact.png and r4/three-hit.gif/mp4 are assembled from the three exported runtime strips. Release7/8 is newly painted as two surviving flow-aligned tongues per hit; those tongues shrink individually and clear on9. No uniform circle, untextured strip, stock flame sprite or repeated recolored asset supplies the effect. Three different silhouettes survive palette removal. Dark/light playback plates reveal readable body/core separation without bloom; these plates are not Minecraft terrain.

Harsh remaining criticism: edges are more shard-like and the interior flow less supple than183036. The 50ms cels and brief holds may feel punchy or may pop in Minecraft; normal-speed asset playback cannot establish final action feel. Two crossed flat carriers remain a camera compromise, not true volume. In-game occlusion, side/back views, motion synchronization, collision stopping, hit results and shader/resource-pack interaction are NOT OBSERVED. These are material limits, not a claim of top-notch finished art.

Next revision after real player evidence: if slash looks like a sticker, change spatial carrier/angle/attachment before adding glow. If release pops, repaint bridging cels using the actual preceding shapes; do not increase random particles. User rejection remains authoritative; no candidate has been marked USER_APPROVED.
