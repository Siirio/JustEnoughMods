# Project Rules

- Preserve current SchemaVersion 2 saves, historical unique defeats, arena data, rematch semantics and global tier synchronization during balance updates because the existing world and its progression must survive launcher changes.
- Bosses created with spawn eggs are legitimate first-kill World Tier encounters because the owner uses eggs for progression testing; `/kill` may finish an already validated first encounter for rapid testing, while command-spawn provenance remains ineligible.
- Completed authoritative boss advancements must reconcile World Tier immediately because waiting for login, respawn, or dimension change delays global progression and downstream tier integrations.
- A global World Tier transition awards its advancement to every online player regardless of participation, and login synchronization awards the current tier to players who join later, because progression belongs to the world rather than individual players.
- Every boss enrolled in the production World Tier roster contributes exactly one unique defeat to global progression, because a visible tier-scaled boss must never be a progression dead end.
- The Progression enchantment gains power only through World Tier III; transitions III to IV and IV to V add no further bonus, and the player-facing book description must state that cap explicitly.
- Progression is acquired and applied only through enchanted books, never offered directly on equipment by the enchanting table; accept modded melee and ranged equipment through vanilla weapon inheritance or a positive native main-hand attack-damage contract, and reserve explicit exclusions for mechanically incompatible equipment, because exact class checks blocked valid Epic Fight, Nightfall, and Cataclysm weapons.
- Ordinary hostile scaling changes the actual max-health and attack-damage attributes at World Tier III, IV, and V and immediately reconciles loaded mobs on a tier transition; registered or tagged bosses are excluded because their native and boss-profile scaling must never stack with the ordinary-mob layer.
- Progression equipment and registered boss-reward equipment retain normal durability, breaking, Unbreaking, and Mending behavior; protection prevents accidental item-entity loss through despawn, fire, lava, or the void without making equipment unbreakable.
- When multiple Progression armor defenses apply to one hit, the strongest applies at full strength and each additional applicable defense contributes only one quarter of its normal strength, because secondary enchantments must remain rewarding without producing excessive stacked mitigation.
- Every defeated registered boss arena in the Overworld, Nether, or End that lacks a native per-boss revival strategy schedules a rematch after 24,000 Overworld game ticks; decide ownership from each BossProfile rather than its mod namespace, preserve Cataclysm respawners, Shell Horn, native eggs, and other declared native mechanics, use one event-driven priority queue plus chunk-load wakeups, never periodic boss/world/chunk scans, rebuild historical defeated-arena schedules from the current Overworld time when the server starts, and do not add a persistence schema solely for the queue.
- Respawned prerequisite bosses never relock MAIN encounters because all prerequisite checks use historical first-defeat flags rather than current entity presence.
- Operator structure recovery does not require a historical defeat: when no living registered boss remains in its arena, `/smp boss revive` replaces stale encounter identity at the native spawn and preserves first-kill progression without unlocking Raid early.
