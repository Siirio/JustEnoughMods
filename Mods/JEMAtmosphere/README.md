# JEM Atmosphere

JEM Atmosphere is the production client-only atmosphere renderer for JustEnoughMods on Forge 1.20.1 and Embeddium 0.3.31.

It owns wind trails, biome fog and haze, sky colors, water mist, cave air, snow gusts, falling leaves, campfire plumes, aurora, rainbow, underwater haze and refraction, heat shimmer, and distant lightning flashes. One Embeddium page and `/jematmosphere settings` expose the master and per-effect controls.

The module does not replace rain, snow, sandstorm, or red-sandstorm rendering. Particle Rain and the native weather state keep that authority. The module contains no terrain lighting, block lighting, entity lighting, or shadow renderer.
