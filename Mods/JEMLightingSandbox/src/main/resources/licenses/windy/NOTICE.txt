The wind atlas contains artwork from Windy by BonfireMC Studios and
the Windy contributors, including project artist bunnua.

Upstream: https://github.com/BonfireMC-Studios/Windy
Revision: cc38fbc5576a7ed9f17fcbb60924aec1923e7e91
Source: https://github.com/BonfireMC-Studios/Windy/archive/cc38fbc5576a7ed9f17fcbb60924aec1923e7e91.zip

The atlas is licensed under LGPL-3.0-only. The LGPL and GPL license texts
are included with this notice. The license of the JEM renderer is separate.
The atlas retains upstream strong-wind pixels and assembles a calm-wind
animation from the original icon's light silhouette. The blue background
is omitted. No upstream Windy Java code is included.

The editable atlas and Aseprite assembly script are in
Mods/JEMLightingSandbox/art/wind in the JustEnoughMods source repository.
